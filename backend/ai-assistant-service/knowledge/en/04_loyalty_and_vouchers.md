# Clucky Club Loyalty and Voucher Guide

## Points and Membership Tiers
Signed-in Clucky members earn points and unlock tier perks:
- **Earning & Redeeming (default policy)**: Earn **1 point per 10,000 VND** of eligible spend (after tier discount & point redemption); **1 point = 1,000 VND** when redeemed on future orders. (Exact rates are governed by Clucky's active loyalty policy.)
- **Membership Tiers** (based on `lifetime_points`):
  - **MEMBER**: Base membership tier.
  - **SILVER**: From **1,000 lifetime points** — **3% tier discount** on eligible orders.
  - **GOLD**: From **5,000 lifetime points** — **5% tier discount** on eligible orders.
- Redeemed points are reserved upon order placement and settled when the order reaches `COMPLETED` (or released if cancelled).

## Vouchers and Promotions
- **Public Web Vouchers**: Listed on the Offers page and applicable at checkout, subject to branch scope, minimum order value, and active dates.
- **Personal Vouchers**: Signed-in customers can claim eligible personal campaigns into their voucher wallet.
- **Counter / Printed Vouchers**: Printed batch vouchers are redeemed in person at a Clucky branch counter (POS) and cannot be claimed as public web codes.
