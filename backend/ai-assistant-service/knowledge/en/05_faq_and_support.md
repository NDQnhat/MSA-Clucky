# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) — Clucky

## Can guests order without signing in?
- **Yes.** Guests can browse the menu, add items to their cart, preview totals, and place orders, as well as chat with the Clucky Assistant about menu items, branch pricing, best sellers, promotions, and store locations.
- Signing in as a Clucky customer is required to **check your personal order history**, **earn/redeem Clucky Club points**, and **claim personal vouchers**.

## How do I see branch-specific prices and availability?
- Select your preferred branch on the Menu page (`/menu`). Branch pricing (`storePrice`) and sold-out status (`isSoldOut`) update automatically for that store.

## How can I check spice level, calories (kcal), options, and toppings?
- Every dish lists calories (`kcal`), spice level (`0` = non-spicy, `1` = mild, `2` = medium, `3` = hot), customizable option groups (Size, Spice Level, Ice/Sweetness, etc.), and optional toppings with transparent prices.
- Ask the Clucky Assistant for things like *"cheapest items"*, *"refreshing summer drinks"*, *"non-spicy food for kids"*, *"low calorie options"*, or *"best sellers at District 1 branch"*.
